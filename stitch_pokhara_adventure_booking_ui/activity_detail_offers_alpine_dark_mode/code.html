Sarangkot Tandem Paragliding

Ascend high above the Pokhara valley floor riding smooth, reliable Himalayan thermal updrafts rising off the Sarangkot ridge. Drift effortlessly alongside eagles and vultures with unobstructed 360-degree panoramic sights of the sacred fishtail peak Machapuchare (6,993m) and the colossal Annapurna Himalayan range. Every flight adheres strictly to international APPI safety certifications, guided by experienced tandem masters before gently descending to the shores of Phewa Lake.
